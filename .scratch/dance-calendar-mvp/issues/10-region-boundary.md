# Region boundary: Events outside Kassel/Göttingen

Type: grilling
Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

Flyers from Fulda, Gießen and Marburg circulate in the same WhatsApp groups (3 of 17 samples, see [flyer-catalogue.md](../assets/flyer-catalogue.md)). Does the calendar accept Events outside Kassel and Göttingen? If yes, how far (a radius, a list of cities, anything an Organizer posts)? If no, what does the bot answer when such a Flyer arrives, and what happens to a Flyer that mixes in- and out-of-region Events (F16)? Also: where is the line for "partner dance" (reggaeton club nights F01, "Brazilian Party" F04, a church service with dancing F15)?

## Answer

Settled with the human, 2026-10-31.

- **No location limit.** Events from any Venue anywhere are accepted: Organizers may post Festivals from far away. The bot never rejects a Flyer for its location, and mixed Flyers (F16, Gießen/Marburg) go in whole.
- **Dancer default:** without filters in the URL, the overview opens on **Kassel + Göttingen, 50 km**. The radius chips gain **"überall"** beyond 100 km. Showing far-away Festivals by default is deferred until we see how many arrive.
- **Styles are optional.** The fixed list stays at five (Salsa, Bachata, Kizomba, Urban Kiz, West Coast Swing). An Event may have **no style** when the Flyer names none.
  - It shows only while no style chip is selected and is hidden once a style is picked.
  - The bot's summary flags it with ⚠️ "Kein Tanzstil erkannt", but a style is never required.
- **Partner-dance line: trust plus clean-up.** The Terms of Use say "partner-dance Events only". The Admin removes misfits, and removes Organizers who keep posting them; Tips are reviewed by the Admin anyway. The sample edge cases (reggaeton club night F01, "Brazilian Party" F04, church service with Salsa F15) are allowed when an Organizer posts them.
