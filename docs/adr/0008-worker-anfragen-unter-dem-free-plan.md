# Worker-Anfragen unter dem Free-Plan halten

The Worker runs on the free plan, which allows 100,000 requests a day, and came close to it on the day the calendar went live on its own domain. A three-minute `wrangler tail` on 2026-11-26 counted 391 requests, about 192,000 a day: 98.7 % came from two AI crawlers, ClaudeBot (209) and GPTBot (177), almost all to `/` with a different `?m=…&von=…` each (months from 2023 to 2030), the rest to `/karte`. Every filter combination of the calendar is its own URL and links to many more (days, months, map, styles), an endless crawl space; there were no cron, queue or waiting-page requests in the sample. Separately, the upload waiting page refreshed every 2 seconds forever, so one forgotten tab would cost about 43,000 requests a day.

Decision: `public/robots.txt` is a static asset, so serving it costs no Worker request. It keeps crawlers out of every URL with a query (`/*?`) and out of the private and per-Event paths (Admin, Organizer area, confirm and edit pages, Flyers, webhooks, the feed and the map). The calendar with a query also answers `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`, for crawlers that have not re-read robots.txt yet. The waiting page counts its own refreshes in `?n=`: every 2 seconds for 15 tries, then every 5 seconds, and after 35 tries (about two minutes) it stops and offers "Nochmal nachsehen" and "Nochmal hochladen".

The cost: only the plain calendar `/` and the static pages can be found through search engines and AI assistants, not a filtered view; crawlers that ignore robots.txt are not stopped (that would need Cloudflare's bot rules, outside the Worker); an Organizer whose Flyer takes longer than two minutes has to click once more.
