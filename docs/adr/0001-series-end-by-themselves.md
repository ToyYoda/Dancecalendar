# Series end by themselves 12 weeks after their last sign of life

A Series generates real Events 12 weeks ahead, but only up to 12 weeks after its last sign of life: the Organizer reposts the Flyer or edits the Series. Without one, the Series simply runs out. There is deliberately no "still running?" reminder: the bot would have to write first, outside WhatsApp's 24-hour window, which needs paid templates (amended 2026-10-31). We chose this over open-ended Series because Organizers rarely announce that a Series has stopped, and a calendar full of ghost Events would cost Dancers' trust more than an occasional gap costs Organizers a repost. This is deliberate, not a bug: don't "fix" it into Series that run forever.
