# The name is DanceAhead, with a road sign as its mark

Supersedes ADR 0005. "Stepdate" can be read as an allusion to a porn genre ("step-…"), which the maintainer does not want next to a community calendar. The product is now called **DanceAhead**: two English words that everyone reads, as on an American road sign ("Dance Ahead"), and the idea of looking ahead to the next dance. The claim is "wo als Nächstes getanzt wird" / "where to dance next", without a place. The wordmark sets "dance" in the band's light type and "ahead" in Apricot; behind it stands the mark, an Apricot warning diamond with an arrow pointing ahead, which is also the favicon. Apricot becomes a second accent beside Bordeaux: the add line, the active type tab and the chosen span, whose first and last day are small diamonds. The ink couple leaves the calendar's band so nothing covers its controls; it keeps dancing on /ueber. The cost: a third rename, so subscribed iCal feeds show a new name; the domain stays stepdate.de and the Telegram bot stays @StepdateBot until danceahead.de is registered and a new bot is created; the trademark is not yet checked.
