# The name is Stepdate, in the Abendkleid palette

Supersedes ADR 0004. "Taktvoll" and the earlier shortlists (Takt, Pista, Soirée) are ordinary words that describe the service, so they could hardly be registered as a trademark, and the maintainer wants a name that works as a wordmark and with a .dance domain. The product is now called **Stepdate** (stepdate.dance): a coined English word from "step" and "date", dance and calendar in one, with the claim "wo heute getanzt wird" / "where to dance tonight" and no place in either. The mark is a 3×3 week grid in which three cells light up diagonally like the footprints of a step diagram; the wordmark sets "step" in ink and "date" in bordeaux. The palette "Abendkleid" (warm paper, plum ink, bordeaux accent, rosé tint, apricot for steps and event dots) replaces the magenta and the blue-grey neutrals, so the page reads calmer and the Flyers stay the loudest colour. The cost: a second rename in one day, so subscribed iCal feeds and anyone who saw "Taktvoll" see a new name again; the trademark and the domain are not yet checked or registered; the bot handle @DanceCalBot, the repository, the worker and the D1 database keep the old name until the maintainer renames them.
